SKNactive Privacy Policy

Last updated August 31, 2026

Your privacy matters to us, especially when it comes to sensitive information like your skin photos and cycle data. Here's exactly what we do and don't do with your information.

What we collect

When you use SKNactive, we collect the information you provide in the skincare questionnaire, including your skin type, concerns, and any conditions you choose to share. If you opt into cycle tracking, we collect the dates and details you log. If you use our skin analysis feature, we temporarily process your photos to extract skin characteristics.

Your photos are never stored

Any face photos you submit for skin analysis are processed to extract skin data only, then permanently deleted. We do not keep, store, or retain the original images at any point after processing.

Account data

If you create an account, your questionnaire responses, cycle tracking data, and routine history are stored securely and linked to your account so your information carries across sessions and devices. If you use the app as a guest, your data is not saved and will not be recoverable if you switch devices or reinstall the app.

You're in control of your data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly from the app's settings page. This permanently removes your questionnaire responses, cycle tracking history, and routine data from our systems.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information, including cycle data or skin information, with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Affiliate links

SKNactive may include affiliate links to Amazon. If you make a purchase through one of these links, we may earn a commission. This does not involve us collecting or accessing your purchase history or payment information.

Medical disclaimer

SKNactive is a skincare coaching tool, not a medical device or diagnostic service. It does not diagnose medical conditions. If the app flags a potential concern, we'll always recommend you consult a licensed dermatologist or medical professional.

Data security

Your data is stored using industry-standard security practices through our backend provider, Supabase, which includes safeguards like row-level security to keep your information private and accessible only to you. Login sessions are stored using your device's secure encrypted storage.

Your rights

You can request to view, update, or delete your data at any time by contacting us or through your account settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SKNactive grows. We'll notify users of material changes.
